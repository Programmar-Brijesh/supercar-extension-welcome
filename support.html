📖 Quick Troubleshooting

  • 🔹 Wallpaper not loading?
    Check your internet connection. Videos are bundled locally, but first load may need cache.
  • 🔹 Voice commands not working?
    Ensure microphone permission is granted. Go to Chrome Settings → Privacy → Microphone.
  • 🔹 Widget positions reset?
    Clearing browser data also clears extension storage. Use "Reset Dashboard" instead.
  • 🔹 Extension not showing in incognito?
    Go to chrome://extensions → Supercar Wallpaper → Details → Allow in Incognito.
